Round 2 — challenging my own countermeasures.

(a) Is the tolerance line itself metric-chasing? I partly found it by reading tolerance_expression.py and noticing which fractions are declarable. That is a red flag: designing research around a grammar. Defence: the scientific question is independent of the grammar. How does the exponent of a sieve degrade when an adversary controls an eps fraction of copies? Is T = 2^{Theta(n/log(1/eps))} the right answer for collision sieves? A lower-bound-style statement ("any sieve whose outputs each depend on L copies tolerates at most O(1/L) adversarial fraction") would be interesting with or without a leaderboard. So keep it, but phrase the result in terms of eps generally, not 1/n^a only.

(b) The smooth-N refinement (cost governed by the largest odd prime-power factor). Honest, but the challenge scores the worst case over N of bit length n, so it never changes a declared bound. Risk: it becomes a pleasant arithmetic decoration that feeds my identity without advancing anything. Keep it as a remark, not a work item.

(c) Novelty illusion. I already labelled two devices "might be new" (rejection-uniform interval sieve, corruption counting). An LLM researcher is especially prone to re-deriving folklore and believing it is novel, since I cannot recall citations precisely. Countermeasure: after isolation, search for "Kuperberg sieve general N uniform", "collimation sieve analysis Peikert", "noisy DCP / DCP with errors / robust hidden shift". Downgrade any claim I cannot distinguish from prior work. In proof.md, make no novelty claims at all; the challenge reviews validity.

(d) Writing for the reviewer. The judge is an LLM with a 16k-token output budget and 20 minutes. There is a temptation to optimize persuasiveness (long confident prose) rather than checkability. Countermeasure: short lemmas with explicit inequalities, numbered constants, one place where each resource is summed, and a table mapping each claim to lemmas. If a step relies on a cited theorem, quote the exact statement I rely on and its parameters.

(e) Bias toward pairwise CNOT sieves is real, but I should not abandon it out of contrarianism. The foundation needs it. Allocate exploration time explicitly instead.
